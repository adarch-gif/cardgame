Model limitations
This is a planning model, not tax-return software or individualized investment advice. Future tax law, ACA rules, Medicare thresholds, returns, inflation, longevity, and healthcare costs can differ materially. Monte Carlo uses seeded lognormal annual return paths with user-selected volatility; it does not forecast markets.
